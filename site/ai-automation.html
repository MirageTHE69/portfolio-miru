01

Answers every channel

Click-to-WhatsApp ads, the website widget, and a follow-up text to anyone whose call you missed. One thread per patient.

02

Asks what you'd ask

Service, preferred day and time, branch, whether it is urgent, and any prep your treatment needs. Your script, not a generic bot's.

03

Holds the slot

Reads live availability, holds a time against your rules, and releases it if the patient goes quiet. No double bookings.

04

Escalates properly

Medical questions, complaints and anything urgent go to a human immediately, with the thread attached. The agent never diagnoses or quotes off-list.

05

Reminds and recovers

A reminder the day before, a reschedule offer if they cancel, and a follow-up to anyone who never showed.

06

Reports weekly

Leads answered, booked, escalated and lost, split by campaign, so you know which ads are actually filling the chairs.
